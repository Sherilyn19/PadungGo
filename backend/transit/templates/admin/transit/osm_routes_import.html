Creates whole routes that OpenStreetMap contributors have mapped, for places where our imported data has no stops (for example parts of Lapu-Lapu and north Mandaue). Stops, their order, the road distances and the road shape all come from that map. Nothing is invented. A route code that already exists from another source is never added again. Routes are stored as documented, not field-verified, and fares stay unknown until a verified fare rule exists. You see everything below before anything is saved.
