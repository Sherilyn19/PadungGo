Riders often change jeepneys a short walk away, such as across the street, not at the exact same stop. Today only the exact same stop counts, so good trips are missed. This adds one-way walking links between stops of different routes that are up to {{ max_walk_m }} m apart (each stop gets its nearest {{ per_stop }} at most).

Nothing is invented: the distance is the straight line between two stops already in the data, the links are stored as documented, and their note says the walking path is not verified. Links that already exist are not added again, so it is safe to run again after new routes are added.
