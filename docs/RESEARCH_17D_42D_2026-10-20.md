## Concrete implications for PadungGo

- Fare Unknown has several possible causes: the planner requires a verified matching vehicle-class fare rule, valid dates, and a supported distance. A published minimum alone cannot establish a whole journey fare. Do not load a flat PHP14 or PHP17 for every journey.
- Record announced fare policies with their effective date and sources in a review queue. Obtain the actual applicable matrix before encoding fractional-distance treatment, rounding or concession amounts.
- Route display names can be curated as documented descriptive labels with source attribution. Do not replace unknown stop IDs using route-level landmark lists.
- Match each unnamed archived stop to coordinates and direction before assigning a landmark. Prefer a transparent fallback such as “Unnamed boarding point” until supported. A nearby landmark is not automatically a legal boarding point.
- Retain the historic/not-field-verified warning for archived geometry. Internet confirmation of a route code cannot verify every archived path or stop. If newer evidence is added, show its specific scope and date separately.
- Next evidence needed from LTFRB Region VII, Cebu City Transportation Office, Talisay traffic office or the actual operator: current 17D/42D authorization and operating status, fleet class/operator, each directional street path, boarding bays and the September 2026 fare matrix. No outreach was sent.

The current repository's `docs/DATA_VERIFICATION.md` requires identity, service, directions, stops, fares, evidence and reuse checks before promoting a route to verified. This research supports better labels and a fare-policy review, but does not satisfy every check.

### Checks on the archived feed (2026-10-20)

Archived 42D and 17D ride geometry from the production API was compared with OpenStreetMap street names (reverse geocoding; data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Results are in `docs/DEMO_JOURNEY_17D_42D.md`, section 7. In short:

- **42D:** follows N. Bacalso Avenue, and the downtown loop runs along Tres de Abril Street about 120 m from Tabo-an Public Market. It only *touches* V. Rama Avenue (about 20 m at its southern end). It starts in Bulacao, Cebu City, and **does not reach Poblacion Talisay**.
- **17D demo leg:** runs Gorordo Avenue → N. Escario Street → Osmeña Boulevard → Fuente Osmeña → Osmeña Boulevard → Sanciangko Street. That is consistent with the feed name ("via Escario") and with source 13 ("via Osmeña Boulevard").

### Fare conclusions

- **Confirmed by sources 11 and 12:** the regular and discounted amounts above, effective 2026-09-28, including in Cebu. The ₱13 and ₱15 minimums from before September 28 are superseded for trips on or after that date.
- **Arithmetic check:** the published discounted minimums equal 80% of regular rounded to the nearest ₱0.25 (₱11.20 → ₱11.25, ₱13.60 → ₱13.50). The discounted per-km amounts are exactly 80% and unrounded (₱1.60, ₱1.92). This is consistent with 25-centavo rounding of the final fare, but it doesn't prove it.
- **Still needed:** the LTFRB **temporary fare guide** for September 2026 that source 12 mentions. It should settle how part kilometres are charged and how fares are rounded, and whether any modern sub-class has different rates.

### Photo of a posted "PUJ General Fare Guide" (supplied by a contributor, 2026-10-20)

- **What it is:** a printed sheet inside a jeepney with "14D" handwritten on it, so not 17D or 42D. It is headed "LTFRB, East Avenue, Quezon City; Effective September 28, 2026". It says fares use the "add-on method" and are "rounded-off to the nearest 25 centavos", and it lists whole kilometres 1–50 for old and new regular and discounted fares.
- **Not usable as the official guide.** It contradicts itself and source 11:

| Problem | Example on the sheet |
|---|---|
| Footer contradicts the table and source 11 | Says ₱2.60 per succeeding km and a ₱13.20 discounted minimum. The table and source 11 use ₱2.00 and ₱11.25. |
| Fares go down as distance goes up | 6 km ₱18.00, then 7 km ₱16.00; 5 km shows ₱14.00; 10 km and 11 km are both ₱26.00 |
| Discounted fare equal to or above regular | 7 km ₱16.00 / ₱16.00; 15 km ₱38.00 regular / ₱38.50 discounted |
| Amounts that break its own 25-centavo note | 18 km discounted ₱33.55 |
| Rows missing or mislabelled | The left column jumps from 23 to "50"; rows 24, 25 and 43 are absent |

- **What it does show:**
  - Rows 1–4 (₱14.00 / ₱11.25) match source 11.
  - Many rows (6, 8, 9, 10, 12–14, 17–19 and most of 26–38 km) equal ₱14 + ₱2 × (km − 4).
  - It lists **whole kilometres only**, so it doesn't settle how 4.055 km is charged.
- **Conclusion:** this is field evidence that a guide with errors is posted in at least one unit. Don't use it for fare rules. Don't commit the photo unredacted, because it shows a plate number.
