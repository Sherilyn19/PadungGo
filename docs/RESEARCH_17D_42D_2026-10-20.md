## Concrete implications for PadungGo

- Fare Unknown has several possible causes: the planner requires a verified matching vehicle-class fare rule, valid dates, and a supported distance. A published minimum alone cannot establish a whole journey fare. Do not load a flat PHP14 or PHP17 for every journey.
- Record announced fare policies with their effective date and sources in a review queue. Obtain the actual applicable matrix before encoding fractional-distance treatment, rounding or concession amounts.
- Route display names can be curated as documented descriptive labels with source attribution. Do not replace unknown stop IDs using route-level landmark lists.
- Match each unnamed archived stop to coordinates and direction before assigning a landmark. Prefer a transparent fallback such as “Unnamed boarding point” until supported. A nearby landmark is not automatically a legal boarding point.
- Retain the historic/not-field-verified warning for archived geometry. Internet confirmation of a route code cannot verify every archived path or stop. If newer evidence is added, show its specific scope and date separately.
- Next evidence needed from LTFRB Region VII, Cebu City Transportation Office, Talisay traffic office or the actual operator: current 17D/42D authorization and operating status, fleet class/operator, each directional street path, boarding bays and the September 2026 fare matrix. No outreach was sent.

The current repository's `docs/DATA_VERIFICATION.md` requires identity, service, directions, stops, fares, evidence and reuse checks before promoting a route to verified. This research supports better labels and a fare-policy review, but does not satisfy every check.
