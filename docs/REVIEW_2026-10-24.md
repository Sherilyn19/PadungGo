## Main findings

The repository contains a functioning React/Django transport planner, not a documentation-only starter. It supports directional direct/one-transfer journeys, independent returns, mapped ride lines and numbered steps, local/provider place search, approximate fare choices, scarcity flags, data administration/imports, and optional hosted AI explanations.

The old documentation mixed planned and implemented behavior. It described a private repository, omitted implemented estimates and place normalization, overstated verified coverage and database/search guarantees, and retained obsolete personal schedules and submission checklists. The updated guides separate current behavior, historical evidence, and future work.
